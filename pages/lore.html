The world of Clouds

A story about a combat medic, a missing sister, and a war that changes everything.

The premise

Some answers are worth crossing borders for.

Clouds is a Catgirl from Melitople who has exhausted every legal way to find her missing sister in Rakiden. When war closes the borders, she makes a reckless choice: join a front-line reconnaissance patrol as its combat medic, even though it may never bring her closer to the truth.

The world is still recovering from Rakiden's former empire and the tense peace that followed its collapse.

Story in development · Details may change
Worldbuilding

Two nations. Many unanswered questions.

Melitople

A constitutional monarchy on the western islands, where Clouds begins her search.

Rakiden

A former empire whose authoritarian resurgence threatens the surrounding nations.

The patrol

Clouds, Sera Vey, Ash, and Rowan Vale — a team whose loyalties and histories are still being explored.
