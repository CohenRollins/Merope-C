Sera Vey

Pure Catgirl · Former Rakiden officer

Sera leaves Rakiden after witnessing abuses she can no longer support. She is disciplined, reserved, and deeply familiar with Rakiden military doctrine.

Her former allegiance creates tension within the patrol despite her commitment to her new comrades. Her assignment and background are still being refined.
