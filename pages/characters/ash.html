Ash

Kobold · Sniper

Quiet and observant, Ash provides overwatch for the patrol and rarely wastes words. Divided Kobold territories have left them skeptical of both major powers.

Ash is often the person reminding Clouds that a medic has to stay alive to save anyone else.
