Clouds

Catgirl · Melitople · Combat medic

Compassionate and stubborn, Clouds puts herself in danger to keep others alive. After her sister disappears in Rakiden, civilian channels fail her. She enlists despite knowing military service cannot promise the answers she wants.

Her recon patrol gives her a new sense of purpose and dangerous new relationships. The war gradually forces her to think beyond her own family.

Character information is draft canon; details may change during writing.
